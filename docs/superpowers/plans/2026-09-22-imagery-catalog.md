# Imagery and catalog publication

User requested redesigned product/editorial imagery, audit of every old image, and publication of six draft concepts. User delegated pricing within the current range ($26–118). No real stock or physical specifications were supplied.

- Audit all 22 old JPEGs and every product/gallery association; record mismatch, repetition and corrupt image evidence.
- Generate unique specification-based images for 22 existing products and six new concepts, plus three editorial scenes, using built-in ImageGen. Mark synthetic images as design illustrations.
- Keep curated imagery in a durable override layer so database exports cannot reintroduce mismatched files.
- Publish six concepts in the storefront with designed prices and public detail pages; keep stock at zero and disable checkout until supply is verified. Describe design intent rather than unverified physical specifications.
- Attach editorial art to relevant articles, gift guide and homepage journal; use product-specific imagery where article topics identify materials.
- Verify files, unique product associations, draft-to-published transition, non-purchasable concepts, complete build, and browser rendering.
